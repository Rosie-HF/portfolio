This project focused on productizing actigraphy data within a physiological-parameter system. My work centered on translating raw activity data and algorithm outputs into metric interpretations and text rules that could be displayed in the product interface, called by the reporting system, and understood by users.

Project focus:

- Organized foundational metrics for sleep-wake patterns, daytime activity, sleep continuity and fragmentation, sleep architecture, and sleep staging, clarifying how each metric should be displayed and interpreted in the product
- Added calculation logic, reference ranges, and medical-interpretation language for metrics such as sleep fragmentation index, sleep-architecture proportions, and REM latency
- Researched higher-level rhythm metrics including cosinor analysis, non-parametric circadian rhythm analysis, and periodogram analysis, expanding actigraphy's application boundary in rhythm assessment
- Broke down comprehensive rhythm score, dominant biological period, energy distribution, activity phase, rhythm stability, and day-night contrast into report modules
- Produced metric definitions, calculation logic, judgment rules, report text, and variable-mapping materials to support automatic generation of explainable reports and future product iterations
