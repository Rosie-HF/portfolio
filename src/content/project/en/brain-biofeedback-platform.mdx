This project aimed to build a multi-parameter biofeedback product with a closed loop across assessment, training/therapy, and reporting. My work focused on building the 0-1 medical-product-side solution: clarifying how the system should collect, assess, intervene, provide feedback, and explain results, then breaking that logic into documents and rules that R&D, design, and third-party teams could execute while also filling the content-resource system.

Project focus:

- Organized indication directions, disease/symptom scenarios, and intervention goals, building the mapping between medical mechanisms, physiological metrics, training goals, and functional modules
- Defined core modules for collection, assessment, training/therapy, and reporting, producing software flows, backend requirements, disease labels, plan fields, and report rules
- Integrated multimodal metrics including EEG, PPG, EMG, EDA, and skin temperature into metric definitions, threshold rules, feedback logic, and result-interpretation language
- Led feedback-game module design, producing 20 EEG feedback game concepts, 10 detailed game plans, and real-time metric mappings, while participating in third-party demo testing and feedback
- Completed the psychotherapy, breathing-training, and music-therapy content system, finalizing 8 psychotherapy guidance scripts and 4 breathing-training scripts, organizing 16 therapy/training asset directories and 225 audio/video assets, and calibrating third-party voiceover and edited deliverables
- Built 4 standardized report templates across assessment, therapy, treatment-course, and trend reports, forming interpretation systems for core metrics such as EEG and HRV and archiving the project knowledge base
