This case reflects my capabilities in research support, experimental design, and data analysis. The work covered non-invasive neuromodulation, sleep, cardio-cerebral coupling, interoception, mental health, and ADHD. The focus was not only on organizing materials, but also on participating in research-question breakdown, experimental workflow design, data-result checking, and research-material delivery.

For the sleep multi-parameter taVNS experiment, I led the experimental protocol design, clarifying stimulation parameters, procedures, acquisition metrics, scale tasks, and timing arrangements so that the research question could be translated into an executable experimental workflow.

Project focus:

- Led the design of a sleep multi-parameter taVNS experimental protocol, organizing stimulation parameters, procedures, acquisition metrics, scale tasks, and timing arrangements
- Participated in grant proposals, ethics applications, and other research materials, helping structure research significance, experimental workflows, research logic, and implementation plans
- Used R for data organization, statistical analysis, and result checking, while contributing to maintenance and updates of the team's statistical-analysis platform
- Took responsibility for literature retrieval, material organization, and selected manuscript writing, supporting research background, theoretical framing, results discussion, and research-output delivery
- Created and delivered statistical-analysis training content, providing the team with R-based statistical methods, data-analysis workflows, and online training support
- Supported research work related to a randomized clinical trial of taVNS, with related results accepted by Molecular Psychiatry
