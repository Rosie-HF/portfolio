---
title: Are these cases more research-oriented or more product-oriented?
---

My experience includes both research and product work, but this portfolio is organized more from a product perspective.

The research experience provides the methodological foundation, including literature retrieval, study design, data analysis, result interpretation, and scientific writing. The product cases emphasize how those capabilities can be translated into buildable product solutions, module designs, report content, and delivery documents.

These cases are therefore not simply a list of research I have worked on. They show how I translate medical and neuroscience evidence into solutions that products can use, R&D can implement, and users can understand.
