This site mainly presents my capabilities in medical product translation, digital therapeutics/biofeedback solution design, research-evidence support, and metric-reporting system development.

The core message is that I can translate medical literature, disease mechanisms, physiological metrics, and clinical scenarios into product frameworks, functional rules, content plans, reporting systems, and R&D-executable materials.

Key capabilities include:

- Translating medical evidence into 0-1 product plans
- Designing closed loops across assessment, training/therapy, feedback, and reporting
- Productizing physiological metrics, algorithm logic, interface presentation, and medical interpretation
- Coordinating standardized delivery across product, R&D, design, algorithms, and external vendors
